- **v2** (m0r0-492f87ba): 2/6 ✅ via `bfs_state_space`

## Observations

- Movement game with mirror / reflection mechanic
- ACTION1-4 move; obstacle layout forces path discovery
- BFS over state hashes resolves up to L2 on both v1 and v2

## Mechanics Hypothesis

Navigate through mirrored/rotated environments where the player's action mapping may effectively flip. BFS captures the deterministic transitions.

- Frame-only solver generalizes across version hashes. See linked lessons for why frame-observation strategies are preferred.

- **script25 adapter attempted (R56, 2026-07-15, `src/admorphiq/adapters25/
  m0r0.py`).** A PoC version composing undirected frontier-BFS (walk
  toward the nearest cell with an untried action, no declared goal) proved
  the kernel-composition plumbing but never reached a target: 0/6 at
  ~4000 actions on a full-budget VM run, hypothesized as a budget
  ceiling since the legacy `bfs_state_space` solver above needed ~2130
  actions for its own 2/6. That hypothesis was FALSIFIED (commit
  `4d9472f`, same round): a goal-directed backport (dc22/ka59's
  optimistic-passability + shortest-path pattern) still measures 0/6 at
  BOTH 500 and 3000 actions — 6x the legacy budget, still zero. Gold-trace
  investigation for the backport found the actual goal is a MOVING
  mirror-partner region (the avatar's own colour used by two
  simultaneously-moving regions; win = the step bringing them into
  pixel-adjacency), re-read from the live frame every planning call
  rather than a static marker — this is a genuinely different goal model
  than the legacy `bfs_state_space` solver's implicit state-hash search
  needed to discover. See [[../rounds/r56_generic-kernels]]'s "Measured
  so far" (m0r0 PoC) and Open items (backport falsification) sections for
  the full writeup; the wall is now diagnosed as a planning/goal-detection
  bug, not budget, and is not yet further isolated.
- **L1 desync-maze fully ISOLATED and BANKED (R56 depth, 2026-07-15,
  gold-replay divergence).** The current joint-state planner (`b9b35cd`)
  clears L0 only (**1/6 @ 0.0057**, re-measured this session). L1 (gold
  `data/traces/m0r0.npz` level_index 1, cleared by gold in 40 actions —
  the only oracle; there is NO L2+ gold) was decoded frame-by-frame:
  - **Control scheme**: two mirror players (colour-10, 4×4 blocks).
    COLUMNS are antisymmetric (ACTION1 diverges the pair, ACTION4
    converges it); ROWS are symmetric (ACTION2/3 move BOTH the same way).
    HUD noise to mask: colour-0 corner counter-bars grow 1→17px along the
    top/bottom edges (an earlier tracker locked onto them by mistake);
    colour-6 (cols 0-31) and colour-15 (cols 32-63) are the two traversed
    ZONES, not walls (players move through them); colour-8 is a 200+ dot
    maze texture.
  - **Winning maneuver (gold)**: fully DIVERGE columns to 10/50 to reach
    the descent door, then EXPLOIT a wall to desync rows (rg 0→4, one side
    blocked while the other moves) so the pair fits offset maze doors,
    descend ~40px to row 46, then re-sync to rg=0. **Win = rg==0 while
    cg is pinned at its wall-capped floor (8px here) at a deep row.**
  - **Why every gap-objective planner stalls (MEASURED, two variants)**:
    the descent doors sit at column offsets that require *increasing* the
    mirror column-gap, but a greedy `gap < current` objective structurally
    forbids diverging. Baseline greedy planner: **0 L1-clears in 1113 L1
    actions**. A merge-goal joint BFS (goal = pieces adjacent, gap gate
    removed, budget 20k): **also 0 L1-clears in 2500 actions** — optimistic
    passability plans in-place convergence and only learns walls reactively,
    so it never proactively diverges to hunt a descent door.
  - **Divergence / stop-rule hit**: the win state IS frame-readable
    (rg==0 & cg==floor), but the PATH is a ~65k-state joint (self×partner)
    maze whose descent doors are not derivable from any frame scalar and
    are only learnable by exhaustive reactive wall-mapping — "the joint
    space explodes past a bounded search" (team stop rule). Banked. Floor
    1/6 preserved (no logic touched). **Reopen** would need either the
    per-piece wall map read from the frame (not cleanly separable here —
    players traverse the colour zones) or a non-greedy joint planner that
    proactively branches on blocked-move desync, gold-generated for L2+.
