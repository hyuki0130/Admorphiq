## Lessons Learned

- Frame-only solver generalizes across version hashes. See linked lessons for why frame-observation strategies are preferred.
- [[../lessons/frame_diff_as_probe]]
- **script25 adapter attempted (R56, 2026-07-15, `src/admorphiq/adapters25/
  m0r0.py`).** A PoC version composing undirected frontier-BFS (walk
  toward the nearest cell with an untried action, no declared goal) proved
  the kernel-composition plumbing but never reached a target: 0/6 at
  ~4000 actions on a full-budget VM run, hypothesized as a budget
  ceiling since the legacy `bfs_state_space` solver above needed ~2130
  actions for its own 2/6. That hypothesis was FALSIFIED (commit
  `4d9472f`, same round): a goal-directed backport (dc22/ka59's
  optimistic-passability + shortest-path pattern) still measures 0/6 at
  BOTH 500 and 3000 actions — 6x the legacy budget, still zero. Gold-trace
  investigation for the backport found the actual goal is a MOVING
  mirror-partner region (the avatar's own colour used by two
  simultaneously-moving regions; win = the step bringing them into
  pixel-adjacency), re-read from the live frame every planning call
  rather than a static marker — this is a genuinely different goal model
  than the legacy `bfs_state_space` solver's implicit state-hash search
  needed to discover. See [[../rounds/r56_generic-kernels]]'s "Measured
  so far" (m0r0 PoC) and Open items (backport falsification) sections for
  the full writeup; the wall is now diagnosed as a planning/goal-detection
  bug, not budget, and is not yet further isolated.
