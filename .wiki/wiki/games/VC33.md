# VC33

> Frame-observation solver; L0 cleared super-human-efficiently by picking the winner of two decoy-paired regions via its smaller containing panel and committing to it exclusively.

## R56 iteration 2 — L0 efficiency (2026-07-15)

VC33 shows two identically-sized colour-9 regions: a WINNER (~3 clicks clears
the level) and a DECOY (clicking it INFLATES the winner's requirement). The
prior adapter probed every candidate once (touching the decoy → inflation)
then round-robined → L0 in 54 actions. **Fix, VERIFIED LIVE:** the winner is
the region nested in the SMALLER containing panel (368 vs the decoy's 848) —
clicking ONLY it clears L0 in exactly 3 clicks, never touching the decoy.
`_region_candidates` now orders by ascending containing-panel size (nested
targets first, winner leads; non-nested candidates keep their old rarest-
colour order), and a lead-commit phase clicks `candidates[0]` exclusively
before probing anything else. **L0: 3 actions vs a 7-action human → score 1.0
(capped), game_score 0.0006 → 0.0357 (60x).** Floor preserved: if the lead
doesn't clear, it falls through to the original probe-all recycle.

**L1+ banked (no gold oracle):** L1 is also a two-colour-9 layout but the
3-click lead doesn't clear it (human baseline 18 vs 7). The game source shows
the real mechanic is a CONNECT/ALIGN puzzle (`ielczunthe`: win = all tokens
aligned to matching slots via connector clicks); the L0 "click 3x" is a
surface case (one already-aligned connector). `data/traces/vc33.npz` covers
only L0, so L1's connector-chain sequence has no oracle — the documented
reopen, not chased blind.
