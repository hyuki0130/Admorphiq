ROOT CAUSE — GENERIC, AND IT IS NOT ABOUT PEG SOLITAIRE
  A REFUSED DRIVE AND A LAGGING FRAME ARE THE SAME PICTURE, and _sync resolved every one of them
  as a lag. Its history test accepts any frame reproducing a state the tool has already left, and
  the state before a drive is always one of those. So a board saying "the carts did not move" was
  dismissed as an animation one action behind; the model marched its carts down track that does not
  exist (pixel rail-reading is over-inclusive — floor drawn in the track's colour reads as track —
  and m.rails was union-only, so nothing could ever retract a cell); and the same refused drive
  came back forever. This is the measured 73 ACTION3 presses, and the coordinator's whole-level
  figure of 359 inert actions in 500.

THE REPAIR AND WHAT IT MOVED (_settle_drive)
  Read the last drive's verdict off the first SETTLED frame, judged on the WINDOW only (a cart
  scrolled off screen is absent for reasons that have nothing to do with the drive). Put the whole
  state back — pieces, carts and cargo. Retract the cell ahead as track only on a SECOND identical
  refusal, because one refusal is indistinguishable from an action swallowed by an animation.

    known cells      61 -> 61 (flat)     becomes   61 -> 99      the map GROWS, first time ever
    drive:empty      239                 becomes   1
    drive:with-passenger 10              becomes   43
    plan:local-win-refused 26,955        becomes   3,099
    ONE retracted rail cell (drive:refused 2, drive:track-retracted 1) redirected the whole level.

  ⛔ The level still does not clear. Per rule 7o a measurement of a MECHANISM does not license a
  change of BEHAVIOUR — this is banked on the gate, not on the count above.

A DEFECT I INTRODUCED AND THE MEASUREMENT THAT CAUGHT IT
  The first _settle_drive restored m.carts and NOT m.pieces. A cart carries its passenger, so the
  piece was left standing where the ride would have taken it. The tell was railhead:nobody-aboard
  = 8 — at every barren moment on the level the model had nobody on any cart, which is impossible
  on a board it had just ridden. Fixed by restoring the whole state.

WHAT STOPPED IT NEXT, AND THE TIER BUILT FOR IT
  After the ride the tool reaches the edge of its own map and every tier reports no gain — which is
  CORRECT on the map it has. Novelty is measured over KNOWN cells, so the one destination worth the
  journey scores exactly zero, and _shunt rolls a cart only onto a cell already called track, so
  every ride stops one cell short of the frontier.
    * _rail_reach now scores a component whose track runs off the SCREEN above every known cell;
    * railhead_moves drives a LADEN cart at that gap — the one move the simulation cannot plan.
  Both are gated on the track COMING FROM the opposite side, or any rail touching the window edge
  reads as heading out of it in all four directions, including straight up.
  This is safe only because a refused drive is now read: a wrong guess costs two actions and is
  never repeated.
